# NeuroQuest Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 10 September 2026_

NeuroQuest is an educational game that teaches how neural networks work. This policy explains
what the app does with your data. The short version: **it does not collect any.**

## What the app stores

NeuroQuest keeps your game progress (levels completed, stars earned, which briefings you have
read) **only on your device**, in the app's private local storage. Nothing is uploaded. If you
delete the app, that progress is deleted with it.

## What the app does not do

- It does not create accounts or ask for your name, email, or any personal information.
- It does not collect analytics, crash reports, or usage statistics.
- It does not show advertising or use advertising identifiers.
- It does not use tracking, fingerprinting, or third-party SDKs that collect data.
- It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, or files.
- It does not connect to the internet. All game content, code, and fonts ship inside the app.

## Children

NeuroQuest is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, no information about
children is collected either.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes what it does with data (for example, adding
optional cloud sync), this policy will be updated first and the change will be described in the
app's release notes.
